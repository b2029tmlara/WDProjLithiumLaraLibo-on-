Your Piano Practice Plan

Welcome to your piano practice plan! Below is a list of things for you to follow or use as a basis on your journey to become a better piano player.

  • Skill Level: Since you are a player, we recommend starting with basic exercises to build a strong foundation. Focus on scales, arpeggios, and simple pieces that match your skill level.
  • Preferred Genre: As a fan of , try incorporating pieces from that genre into your practice routine. This will keep you motivated and make practice more enjoyable.
  • Practice Time: With a daily practice time of hours, we suggest breaking it down into focused sessions. For example, you could do 30 minutes of technical exercises, 30 minutes of sight-reading, and 1 hour of working on pieces.
